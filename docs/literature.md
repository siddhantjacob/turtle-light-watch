## Preliminary gaps (from abstracts and searches only — CONFIRM after reading)
1. Satellite turtle-light studies cluster in Florida, the Mediterranean and Australia. A search on 2026-10-01 found no VIIRS time-series study of Odisha's arribada beaches (searches are not exhaustive).
2. The main "change over time" study (Kamrowski 2014) appears to rely on the older DMSP satellite; VIIRS-era trends (2014-2026) at mass-nesting beaches look under-studied.
3. Harm is proven in the field at Rushikulya (Karnad 2009), but no one seems to have linked it to a long satellite record.
4. Gahirmatha's nesting moved onto an island with a lit facility (Behera 2016); no satellite light assessment of that shift found.
5. The LED blind spot (Kyba 2023) is rarely handled explicitly in turtle studies — check how papers 1, 3 and 6 treat it.
