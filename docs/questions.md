# Questions — Dark Skies for Hatchlings

## Why light matters more at arribada beaches (M0)
At arribada beaches, a large share of the region's hatchlings emerge from a few kilometres
of sand within a few nights, so a single nearby light source can disorient a far larger
fraction of the population than it would on a beach with scattered nesting.
